8 content skills, persistent brand memory, and a quality gate that catches AI slop before you ever see it. One command to install, zero config.

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/quionie/claude-content-engine/main/install.sh | bash

or, inside Claude Code: /plugin marketplace add quionie/claude-content-engine

Eight skills, one engine

Ask naturally — Claude picks the right skill and loads your saved voice.

content-repurposer

One piece of content becomes platform-native versions for Twitter, LinkedIn, email, and more. Re-angled, not resized.

blog-post-architect

SEO-structured posts with a differentiation angle — meta descriptions, heading structure, internal links.

copywriting-engine

Headlines, landing pages, and ad copy built on PAS, AIDA, and BAB frameworks.

brand-voice-builder

Feed it your best writing; get a reusable voice profile any future session can follow.

email-sequence-builder

Full drip campaigns — welcome, launch, onboarding, re-engagement — with timing and exit logic.

social-media-calendar

Weeks of scheduled, platform-formatted content mapped to your pillars.

content-workflow

Chains skills into pipelines: voice → blog post → repurposed set → posting calendar, in one run.

content-memory

Your voice, audience, and preferences persist across sessions. Explain your brand once.

The quality gate

Hooks scan everything Claude writes — about 50 patterns across three severity tiers — and feed the findings back so it rewrites before you read it. Add your own banned phrases and they're enforced too.

✓ what ships instead

Built like a tool, not a prompt pack

local-first memory

Everything lives in ~/.claude-content-engine/ on your machine. Delete the folder, it's gone.

tested + CI

The hooks ship with a real test suite, run on every push. MIT licensed, markdown all the way down.

yours to edit
